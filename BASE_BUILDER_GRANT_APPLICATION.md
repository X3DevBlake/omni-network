## 📝 Application Questions & Answers

### 1. What is your project, and what does it build on Base?
> **Answer**:  
> OMNI is a high-throughput decentralized ecosystem integrating AI compute routing, decentralized exchange (DEX) liquidity protocols, and automated gaming economies. We are expanding to **Base** to leverage its sub-cent gas fees and seamless Coinbase fiat on-ramps. Our public contract implementation (`OmniTokenPublic.sol`) features EIP-2612 gasless permits, fixed supply governance, deflationary burn mechanics, and a native Uniswap V3 concentrated liquidity AMM pool on Base.

### 2. Why are you choosing to build on Base?
> **Answer**:  
> Base is the premier Ethereum Layer 2 with deep DeFi liquidity, an active developer ecosystem, and native alignment with the Superchain. Building on Base allows our users to execute micro-transactions, DEX swaps, and cross-chain bridging with millisecond latency and minimal gas costs, while enabling seamless onboarding for mainstream users through Coinbase.

### 3. What will the gas grant / developer credits be used for?
> **Answer**:  
> The gas grant will be used directly for:
> 1. Deploying and verifying the core `OmniTokenPublic.sol` contract on Base Mainnet.
> 2. Initializing and seeding the official Uniswap V3 concentrated liquidity pool (`OMNI / USDC`) on Base.
> 3. Sponsoring initial user onboarding transactions and gasless token approvals via Account Abstraction (ERC-4337) and Coinbase Developer Platform Paymasters.
